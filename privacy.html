Privacy Policy

EURA / Last updated November 11, 2026

Eura builds personal clothing stores, called Marts, around your ideas, references, and rules. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website, request a Mart, join the waitlist, or contact us.

Marts are picked by people. When you request a Mart on eura.style, the Eura team reads your request, looks at your references, and chooses the products by hand. Requesting a Mart does not create an app account. Connecting Pinterest is optional.

What we collect

  • Mart requests. When you request a Mart, we store what you write (what you are shopping for, any budget, answers to follow-up questions, and sizes if you add them), your email address, any photos you upload, any Pinterest board links you add, and when you submitted it. If you connect Pinterest, we read the boards you choose (board names and pin images) once, to build your Mart. We ask for read-only access, never post to your account, and delete our access when you submit, or after 24 hours if you don't. The pin images we read are kept with your request. We remove location and camera details from photos when we receive them. We also record which page or ad brought you to the form.
  • Your Mart. When your Mart is ready, we store the products we picked and send you a private link to it. Anyone who has that link can view your Mart, so keep it to yourself.
  • Waitlist details. When you sign up, we store your email address and the time you joined. Both forms on the website send signups to the same list.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to include.
  • Technical information. Our hosting and backend providers process information such as IP addresses, request times, and browser or device details to deliver the website, operate the signup form, and prevent abuse. Signup activity may also appear in server logs.
  • Website usage. The landing page uses Google Analytics to measure visits, referral sources, browser and device information, approximate location, and activity such as viewing the demo, scrolling, and completing a signup. Analytics may use cookies and similar identifiers.

Our signup analytics event records which form was used, without including the email address submitted. The website does not request access to your microphone or camera. It asks for access to your Pinterest account only if you choose Connect Pinterest. Photos reach us only when you choose them in the Mart form.

How we use your information

  • Build your Mart: read your request, look at your references, choose products, and email you when it is ready.
  • Ask a follow-up question about a request, or change a Mart when you reply with feedback.
  • Maintain the waitlist and send Eura updates, early-access invitations, or requests for product feedback.
  • Answer messages and handle requests about your information.
  • Understand interest in Eura and improve the website and product presentation.
  • Operate the website and signup service, diagnose problems, and limit spam or abuse.

We do not sell waitlist addresses or use them to send unrelated third-party promotions. Submitting the waitlist form does not send your email address to an AI model for fashion recommendations. To suggest a follow-up question while you fill in the Mart form, we send the text you type to an AI service (see below). We do not send it your email address or photos. We may use Mart requests and our product choices, without your email address, to improve how we pick products.

Services that process information

We use other companies to deliver the website and manage communications:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Railway hosts our backend and database, where Mart requests, uploaded photos, and waitlist records are stored. See the Railway Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics processes website usage information. Google Fonts also receives the network information needed to serve the site's fonts, such as your IP address and browser request details. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics privacy information.
  • OpenAI receives the text of your Mart request to suggest a follow-up question. See the OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES) sends our emails, including Mart deliveries and request alerts. See the AWS Privacy Notice.
  • Pinterest handles the sign-in and approval when you connect your account. See the Pinterest Privacy Policy.
  • Retailers serve the product images shown on your Mart page, so opening the page sends your browser's network information, such as your IP address, to those retailers. When you follow a product link, the retailer's own privacy policy applies.
  • Email and invitation services process addresses and correspondence when we communicate with you. This includes Google when you contact our Gmail address, and Apple if we send an early-access invitation through TestFlight.

Eura team members can access Mart requests, waitlist records, and messages to carry out the purposes above. We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect against fraud or harm.

Our team is based in the United States, and our providers operate internationally. Information may be processed in the United States or other countries where those providers operate.

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics can use cookies to distinguish visits and measure activity. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on where supported. Blocking analytics does not prevent you from joining the waitlist.

To stop Eura waitlist emails or leave the list, email eurastyleai@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up. You do not need an app account to make this request.

How long we keep information

We keep Mart requests, photos, and your Mart so we can build it, update it, and answer you, until you ask us to delete them or we close the service.

Waitlist records have no automatic expiration. We keep them to manage interest and invitations until you ask us to remove your address or we close the waitlist. We retain correspondence as needed to answer you and keep a record of the request.

Technical logs, analytics records, and provider backups have separate retention settings and schedules. Removing an address from the active waitlist does not instantly erase every backup or provider log. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for those systems.

Access, correction, and deletion

You can ask what information we hold about you, request a copy or correction, or ask us to delete your Mart requests, photos, waitlist record, and messages. Contact eurastyleai@gmail.com. We may need to verify that you control the relevant email address.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to withdraw consent, object to or restrict processing, or complain to your local privacy regulator. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations or security.

Security

The website and our backend use HTTPS to encrypt information in transit. Your Mart link contains a private code; we store only a scrambled (hashed) form of it. No website, database, or email service can guarantee complete security.

Age requirement

Eura is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect Mart requests or waitlist information from children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can remove it.

Future features and earlier versions

Some features shown in our demo, such as connecting Instagram accounts or automatic product updates, are not available yet. Before we collect information for them, we will update this policy to explain what they collect and how.

If you used the earlier voice-based Eura app, the earlier app privacy policy remains available. This website notice does not authorize new uses of information collected under that earlier policy.

Changes and contact

We will update this notice and its date when website practices change, and provide additional notice where required. Questions or privacy requests: eurastyleai@gmail.com.
