Privacy Policy

EURA / Last updated October 26, 2026

Eura is developing a way to create personal clothing stores, called Marts, around your ideas, references, and rules. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website, join the waitlist, or contact us.

Website and waitlist only. The demo illustrates a product in development. Joining the waitlist does not create an app account, connect Pinterest or Instagram, or upload any reference photos. We will provide an app-specific privacy notice before collecting information for a trial of the new Mart experience.

What we collect

  • Waitlist details. When you sign up, we store your email address and the time you joined. Both forms on the website send signups to the same list.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to include.
  • Technical information. Our hosting and backend providers process information such as IP addresses, request times, and browser or device details to deliver the website, operate the signup form, and prevent abuse. Signup activity may also appear in server logs.
  • Website usage. The landing page uses Google Analytics to measure visits, referral sources, browser and device information, approximate location, and activity such as viewing the demo, scrolling, and completing a signup. Analytics may use cookies and similar identifiers.

Our signup analytics event records which form was used, without including the email address submitted. The website does not request access to your microphone, camera, photo library, or social accounts.

How we use your information

  • Maintain the waitlist and send Eura updates, early-access invitations, or requests for product feedback.
  • Answer messages and handle requests about your information.
  • Understand interest in Eura and improve the website and product presentation.
  • Operate the website and signup service, diagnose problems, and limit spam or abuse.

We do not sell waitlist addresses or use them to send unrelated third-party promotions. Submitting the waitlist form does not send your email address to an AI model for fashion recommendations.

Services that process information

We use other companies to deliver the website and manage communications:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Railway hosts our signup backend and database, where waitlist records are stored. See the Railway Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics processes website usage information. Google Fonts also receives the network information needed to serve the site's fonts, such as your IP address and browser request details. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics privacy information.
  • Email and invitation services process addresses and correspondence when we communicate with you. This includes Google when you contact our Gmail address, and Apple if we send an early-access invitation through TestFlight.

Eura team members can access waitlist records and messages to carry out the purposes above. We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect against fraud or harm.

Our team is based in the United States, and our providers operate internationally. Information may be processed in the United States or other countries where those providers operate.

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics can use cookies to distinguish visits and measure activity. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings, or use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on where supported. Blocking analytics does not prevent you from joining the waitlist.

To stop Eura waitlist emails or leave the list, email eurastyleai@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up. You do not need an app account to make this request.

How long we keep information

Waitlist records have no automatic expiration. We keep them to manage interest and invitations until you ask us to remove your address or we close the waitlist. We retain correspondence as needed to answer you and keep a record of the request.

Technical logs, analytics records, and provider backups have separate retention settings and schedules. Removing an address from the active waitlist does not instantly erase every backup or provider log. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for those systems.

Access, correction, and deletion

You can ask what information we hold about you, request a copy or correction, or ask us to delete your waitlist record and messages. Contact eurastyleai@gmail.com. We may need to verify that you control the relevant email address.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to withdraw consent, object to or restrict processing, or complain to your local privacy regulator. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations or security.

Security

The website and signup endpoint use HTTPS to encrypt information in transit. No website, database, or email service can guarantee complete security.

Age requirement

The waitlist is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect waitlist information from children under 16. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can remove it.

The new app and earlier versions

The new Mart concept includes briefs, reference images or links, optional connected sources such as Pinterest, shopping rules, saved products, and feedback. These are demo features, not additional information collected by this website. Before a trial collects such information, its privacy notice will explain the actual data flows, providers, permissions, retention, and deletion choices.

If you used the earlier voice-based Eura app, the earlier app privacy policy remains available. This website notice does not authorize new uses of information collected under that earlier policy.

Changes and contact

We will update this notice and its date when website practices change, and provide additional notice where required. Questions or privacy requests: eurastyleai@gmail.com.
